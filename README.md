The codes used in this study are primarily for data preprocessing, trend analysis, and attribution exploration. The purpose of each Python script is as follows:
1、Mat-to-csv.py: Format conversion of lake surface water temperature data.
2、NC-TIF.py: Preprocessing of climate factor data.
3、Theil-Sen estimator.py: Calculation of lake surface water temperature warming rates.
4、Mann-Kendall.py: Trend analysis of lake surface water temperature warming rates.
5、K-means.py: Classification of lake categories based on urbanization intensity.
6、T-test.py: Significance testing of differences in lake warming rates.
7、Sixfold Buffer Zone of Urban Boundary.py: Construction of the six‑fold urban boundary buffer system.
8、Lake Proximity to Urban Centers.py: Calculation of lake proximity to urban centers.
9、Detection of Lake Heatwaves.py: Detection of lake heatwave events.
10、Detection of Lake Recovery Time.py: Detection of lake heatwave recovery time.
11、Impact of Urbanization on Lake Heatwaves.py: Mediation analysis of urbanization effects on lake warming and heatwaves.
12、Impact of Climate Change on Lake Heatwaves.py: Mediation analysis of climate change effects on lake warming and heatwaves.
13、Investigation of Lake Heatwave Variations.py: Attribution analysis of heatwave differences between urbanized and non‑urbanized lakes.
14、Relative Influence Assessment.py: Partial correlation analysis to isolate the independent effects of urbanization intensity, proximity, and compactness on LSWT.
